# Simple Version (omega oats v1)

## Ingredients

- eggs
- 250 - 300 ml water or bone broth or green tea
- 1/3 cup fiber oat bran or rolled oats
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds and ground flaxseeds
- salt, msg, pepper
[ Microwave for 2-3 min ]
- olive oil
- 0% fat no added sugar Greek yogurt
- raw unsalted unroasted Nuts
	- walnuts and pumkin seeds are healthy

## Recipie

Basically the ingredients is the recipe. 

1. whisk the eggs
2. add pre microwave ingredients
3. microwave, watch for overflow
4. add post microwave ingredients

You can optionally do this without microwaving and instead keep it in the fridge for overnight oats if your into that.

## Recipie

So all these together would be banging, any ratio or quantity of any of these is banging, removing any ingredients is banging, since it's meant to be more as inspiration and you can build with what you have depending on your mood. Selection is the name of the game! And oats is your canvas! 

But if you want it to be more cohesive/coherent, like there is an intention behind the dish. Then you can make it more like risotto or congee, or a mediterranean, or asian or indian style (using cumin)! 

And you don't have to make oats the base of the dish!! You can have eggs be the base, or veggies, or a rice sub!!

you can add any seasoning you want, zaatar, sumac (not so good), kabsa, paprika.

1. The "Oat-Sotto" (Italian Inspired)

_Focus: Creamy, earthy, and rich._

    Pre-Microwave Mix: 
	    1/3 cup Oat Bran, 
	    1 raw egg, 
	    250ml Bone Broth, 
	    1/4 cup frozen riced cauliflower, 
	    1 tbsp Nutritional Yeast, 
	    1/2 tsp mushroom powder, 
	    pinch of garlic powder.

    Microwave: 2–3 minutes (stir halfway).

    Post-Microwave Fold-in: 1 tbsp Greek yogurt (for creaminess).

    Toppings: 
    	Drizzle of Olive Oil, 
	handful of Walnuts, 
	and 2 chopped Sun-dried tomatoes.

    The Finisher: A heavy squeeze of Lemon Juice and cracked black pepper. and PARMAJAAN

2. The "Savory Zen" (Congee Inspired)

_Focus: Clean, warming, and salty-oceanic._

    Pre-Microwave Mix: 
    	1/3 cup Oat Bran, 
	1 raw egg, 
	250ml water, 
	1 tsp Apple Cider Vinegar, 
	1/2 cup frozen Spinach, 
	MSG,
	1/4 tsp ginger powder (or frozen grated ginger).

    Microwave: 2–3 minutes (stir halfway).

    Post-Microwave Fold-in: 1 tsp Miso Paste (mix well until dissolved).

    Toppings: 
	Seaweed sprinkles (Nori/Furikake), 
	Hemp hearts, 
	Kimchi
	and Peanuts.

    The Finisher: A tiny dash of sesame oil (optional) or more seaweed.

3. The "Greek Power Bowl" (Mediterranean Inspired)

_Focus: Bright, briny, and acidic._

    Pre-Microwave Mix: 
	1/3 cup Oat Bran, 
	1 raw egg, 
	250ml water, 
	1/4 cup Canned crushed tomatoes, 
	1/2 cup frozen Kale, 
	pinch of garlic powder.

    Microwave: 2–3 minutes (stir halfway).

    Post-Microwave Fold-in: A large dollop of Greek Yogurt (don't over-mix; keep it chunky like feta).

    Toppings:
	Sliced Olives,
	Pumpkin seeds, 
	and a drizzle of Olive Oil.

    The Finisher: 
	A squeeze of Lemon Juice 
	and a pinch of dried oregano.

4. The "Golden Dal" (Indian Inspired)

_Focus: Fragrant, filling, and anti-inflammatory._

    Pre-Microwave Mix: 
	1/3 cup Oat Bran,
	1 raw egg,
	250ml water/broth, 
	2 tbsp Mashed Cannellini beans, 
	Turmeric, 
	pinch of Cumin (if you have it), 
	curry powder,
	black pepper.

	canned brown lentils or pre cooked bagged lentils
	or basically any indian minute meal

    Microwave: 2–3 minutes (stir halfway).

    Post-Microwave Fold-in: 1 tbsp Greek Yogurt (acts like "raita" to cool the spices).

    Toppings: 
	Cashews, 
	Hemp hearts, 
	and a drizzle of Olive Oil.

    The Finisher: A squeeze of Lemon Juice (essential to brighten the turmeric).

# THEORY OF FOOD / MEALS / DISHES

### My theory of food is simple, you have the major content of the meal, that being 
- Protien
- Veggie
- Base/Carb, but not actual rice but a base that holds it together, be it rice or pasta/noodle or bread or bread wrap

### Then you have the minor content or flavors of the meal, which is the 5 flavors
- Sweet
- Sour
- Savory / Umami
- Salty
- Bitter (optional)

#### If you have protien+veggie+base that covers sweet+sour+savory+salty then you have a tasty and healthy dish.

### Then add spices and sauses to your heart's content!

#### And always use olive oil for everything.
