Savory oats make breakfast feel less sweet but more homey. Toss the honey, banana, fruits to the side, and make this. 
Simple, high fiber shredded oat bran (1/3 cup), 
water (3/4 cup - 1 cup to taste/texture) 
salt, 
msg, 

microwave for 2 min (+- depending on consistancy) 

you might need to open the microwave to stop the oats from rising but then you'll have to run it for longer. 
Then take it out and cool for a few minutes. 
Taste for salt and msg. 
Now add freshly ground pepper 
and with each bite, olive oil. 

Allahu akbar. 

Other than that you can add any seasoning you want, zaatar, sumac (not so good), kabsa, paprika.
