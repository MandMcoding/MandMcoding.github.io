## Sithuni's Sri Lankan style Lentil Recipie

### Lentils by Sinthua
```
    Wash/rinse lentils
    Add water until lentils are completely covered
    Place in pot on medium heat
    Add handful of red onion
    Add minced garlic cloves 1-3
    Add handful of curry leaves
    Add handful of pandan leaves
    Some cinnamon (very small amount)
    2-3 green chilis
    Add Spices: turmeric 1 pinch, SL curry powder 3/4 teaspoon, SL chili powder 1 teaspoon (add more depending on appearance and taste)
    Cover and cook for about 10ish mins
    Once halfway soaked add more water if not fully cooked
    Add 2 tablespoons coconut milk after lentils are fully cooked

*you’ll know they’re fully cooked when all seeds are puffed up

    cook for five more minutes to let milk incorporate


Tempering (you can start while lentils are about halfway done)

    Small pot with vegetable oil (enough to cover the bottom of pot)
    Add ingredients from small white bowl (see photo) onion, 1 garlic clove, curry leaves (not mandatory), mustard seeds 2 teaspoons
    Add onions first (takes longest to cook)
    Once onions cook for a little, add curry leaves and pandan leaves
    Mustard seeds (stand back/be careful)
    Add garlic, one dried red chili, chili powder (3/4 tablespoon) closer to end as they will burn (1 minute of cooking is enough)


    Pour into lentils with special trick to get everything out of pot 😎
    Switch off heat and stir
```
