So I thought, since making the omega oats v1, eggs+water+oats, and they are overhydrated, I wanted to try without water. Then I did;

- 3 Eggs, Whisked / Scrambled
- 1/3 Cup Oats
- Seasonings 

Then I nuked it for like 2-2:30 min, and most importantly, didn't touch it. Online they say to mix it every 15 sec, but for this you want the kibbeh texture.

Then at the end you'll have cooked eggs and oats.

Here's the issue; it's dry as fuq. That's why we add 

1. Olive oil
2. Yogurt!

Then the texture as I was eating it was much more meaty with a nice bite to it, and with yogurt it legit tastes and feels like kibbeh!!!
