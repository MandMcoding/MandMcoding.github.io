# Master Sheet Pan Batch Prep Guide

### Oven Temperature: 200°C (400°F)

---

## Tray 1: The "Long Roasts" (Root Veggies & Meat)
* **Ingredients:** Chicken thighs, sweet potatoes (cubed), carrots (sliced), turkey meatballs, and the garlic bulb (top sliced off).
* **Prep:** Toss everything in oil, salt, pepper, and your choice of herbs (rosemary or thyme work well here).
* **Oven Time:** 35–45 minutes.
* **Tip:** Give the chicken and potatoes space so they crisp up instead of steaming.

## Tray 2: The "Medium Roasts" (Hardy Veg & Plant Protein)
* **Ingredients:** Broccoli, Brussels sprouts, chickpeas, tofu cubes, and artichoke hearts.
* **Prep:** Pat the tofu and chickpeas very dry before oiling. This is the tray for "savory" spices like smoked paprika or garlic powder.
* **Oven Time:** 20–25 minutes.
* **Tip:** You can slide this tray into the oven when Tray 1 has about 20 minutes left.

## Tray 3: The "Quick Finishers" (Delicate Veg & Seafood)
* **Ingredients:** Salmon fillets, shrimp, asparagus, mushrooms, red onions, bell peppers, cherry tomatoes, and lemon halves (face down).
* **Prep:** These cook fast and release a lot of juice. Keep the salmon in the center and surround it with the veggies.
* **Oven Time:** 10–12 minutes.
* **Tip:** Add this tray during the final stretch. The tomatoes and lemons will create a "sauce" you can drizzle over everything else.

---

## The Master Schedule (The "Big Bake")
If you want to manage your time like a pro, follow this countdown:

* **T-Minus 45 Mins:** Put **Tray 1** in the oven.
* **T-Minus 25 Mins:** Put **Tray 2** in on a lower rack. Toss/flip the items on Tray 1.
* **T-Minus 12 Mins:** Put **Tray 3** in.
* **0 Mins:** Pull everything out. Let the meat and salmon rest for 5 minutes before packing away.
