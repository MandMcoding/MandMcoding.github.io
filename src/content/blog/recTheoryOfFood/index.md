# THEORY OF FOOD / MEALS / DISHES

### My theory of food is simple, you have the major content of the meal, that being 
- Protien
- Veggie
- Base/Carb, but not actual rice but a base that holds it together, be it rice or pasta/noodle or bread or bread wrap

### Then you have the minor content or flavors of the meal, which is the 5 flavors
- Sweet
- Sour
- Savory / Umami
- Salty
- Bitter (optional)

#### If you have protien+veggie+base that covers sweet+sour+savory+salty then you have a tasty and healthy dish.
